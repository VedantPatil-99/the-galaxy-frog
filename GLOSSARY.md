# Galaxy Frog

Shared terms for the notebook workspace.

## Language

**Source scope**:
The material a notebook question is allowed to use. It can cover all eligible notebook sources,
specific sources, or selected passages.

**Answer-generation model**:
The model chosen to produce a conversation answer. Embedding and OCR have their own processing
models.

**Source removal**:
Excluding a source from future retrieval in a notebook while preserving historical answers where
access permits.

**Permanent source deletion**:
Erasing a source and removing or invalidating private content that depends on it.
